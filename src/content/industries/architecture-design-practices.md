## 01 / The problem we solve

# Deadlines pile up faster than your team can draw.

Architecture studios bring us in when documentation deadlines collide with design work. We act as an extension of your team, working in your templates so the result looks like it came from your own office.

### What usually gets in the way

- Submission and documentation deadlines arriving at the same time
- Not enough Revit or rendering hands in the office this month
- Design changes that have to ripple through models, sheets and images
- Outside help that ignores your templates and standards

### What you get from us

- Revit models and drawing sets built in your templates and standards
- Drawings kept in step with the latest approved design
- Planning and client visuals that match the current revision
- A clear list of assumptions and open questions at every handoff

## 02 / How a project runs

# Clear brief, steady updates, clean handoff.

### 01 — Agree your standards

We take your templates, naming rules and who approves what.

### 02 — Split the work

We package the job by building, floor, stage or deliverable.

### 03 — Review with your leads

Your design leads review each package before it moves on.

### 04 — Hand back clean files

You receive editable native files plus a short list of open items.

---

## 03 / Who stays responsible

You remain the architect of record. Design authorship, planning responsibility and professional sign-off stay with your practice.
